# Cyclo test builds

Thanks for helping test **Cyclo** by **LeverOne Studios**, the app that scans period care, intimate care and skincare products and rates them from Excellent to Poor.

## Install on Android

1. On your Android phone, open the [latest release](../../releases/latest).
2. Under **Assets**, tap the file ending in **.apk** to download it.
3. Open the downloaded file. The first time, Android will ask you to allow installs from your browser. Tap **Settings**, switch on **Allow from this source**, then go back and tap **Install**.
4. Open Cyclo and scan something!

To update, come back here and install the newest release over the old one. Your history and saved products stay put.

## Feedback

Spotted a bug, a score that looks wrong, or have an idea? Tap **Send feedback** in the app (on the home screen or in Profile). It opens an email with your app version filled in.
